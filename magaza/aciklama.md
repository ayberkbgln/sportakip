yapıldı. Hesap açman gerekmiyor, reklam yok ve verilerin telefonundan çıkmıyor.

**Sana göre kuruluyor**
Kurulum sihirbazı ölçülerini, hedefini ve haftalık programını soruyor. Kalori
ve protein hedefin Mifflin-St Jeor formülüyle hesaplanıyor, istersen elle
değiştiriyorsun.

**Her spor**
Kickboks, boks, MMA, ağırlık, koşu, yüzme, bisiklet, yoga, takım sporları ve
daha fazlası. Bir güne birden çok seans ekleyebilirsin ve sıra korunuyor —
ısınma koşusu, sonra kickboks, sonra ağırlık. Her sporun kendi günlüğü var:
koşuda mesafe ve tempo, dövüşte raund, ağırlıkta set-tekrar-kilo ve set arası
dinlenme sayacı.

**Yemek kaydı**

kısayollarıyla hızlı ekle, listede yoksa kendi yemeğini kaydet — bir dahaki

antrenmanından önce uyarıyor ve aç karnına alınmaması gerekenleri hatırlatıyor.

**Bırakmak istediğin bir şey varsa**
Şekerli içecek, sigara, fast food — haftalık üst sınır koyup her hafta düşüren
bir azaltma takvimi kuruyor.

## Description

Plan keeps your nutrition, workouts and body measurements in one place.
No account to create, no ads to close, and your data never leaves your phone.

**Built around you**
The setup wizard asks for your measurements, your goal and your weekly
schedule. Calorie and protein targets are calculated with the Mifflin-St Jeor
formula — and you can always override them by hand.

**Every sport**
Kickboxing, boxing, MMA, weightlifting, running, swimming, cycling, yoga,
team sports and more. Stack multiple sessions on one day and keep their
order — warm-up run, then kickboxing, then weights. Each sport gets its own
log: distance and pace for runs, rounds for fight sports, set-rep-weight
tracking with a rest timer for the gym.

**Exercise library**
39 exercises with clear step-by-step instructions and simple motion diagrams,
including a home-workout set and a desk-posture routine. Recommendations
adapt to your goal.

**Food logging**
A built-in food database — no internet needed. Quick portion shortcuts,
plus your own custom foods: log something once and it shows up in search
next time. Scan a product barcode once, and the next scan opens it instantly.

**Weekly budget**
One heavy day doesn't reset you. The app spreads what's left of your weekly
budget over the remaining days and tells you your new daily target — the
approach that actually works in real life.

**Measurements**
Body-fat percentage with the U.S. Navy method, using the correct formula for
your sex. Weight trend curve and weekly summaries.

**Supplement warnings**
Save the supplements you take; the app totals your caffeine, warns you before
evening sessions and reminds you which ones shouldn't be taken on an empty
stomach.

**Cutting back on something?**
Sugary drinks, cigarettes, fast food — set a weekly cap and follow a
gradually decreasing schedule, week by week.

**Made for iPhone**
Two-way Apple Health sync, iCloud sync across your devices, water and
training reminders.

**Privacy**
We have no server. No analytics, no ads. Your data lives on your device and
in your own iCloud account — we can't see any of it.

The interface is available in English and Turkish.

This app does not provide medical advice. Nutrition values are approximate
reference figures.
